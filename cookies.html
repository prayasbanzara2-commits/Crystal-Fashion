Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can control them.

Last updated: June 2026 · This policy explains how Crystal Fashion uses cookies and similar technologies on crystalfashion.com.np.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet or phone when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

Similar technologies we may use include local storage, session storage, and pixel tags. For simplicity, this policy refers to all of these collectively as "cookies".

2. Why We Use Cookies

Crystal Fashion uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential functioning — to make our site work properly (for example, remembering that you accepted the cookie banner).
  • Preferences — to remember your choices, such as language or filter selection.
  • Analytics — to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.
  • Embedded content — third-party services like Google Maps, Google Fonts, QR-code generation, and Formspree may set their own cookies when their content loads on our pages.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the site to function. They cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions you take such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in a form.

4. How to Control Cookies

You have several ways to control or limit how cookies are used on our site:

  • Cookie banner — when you first visit our site, you can choose "Accept" or "Decline" via the banner that appears at the bottom of the screen.
  • Browser settings — most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies. Each browser is different; check the Help menu of your browser, or visit allaboutcookies.org for step-by-step instructions.
  • Clear local storage — to reset your consent, clear your browser's site data for crystalfashion.com.np.

Please note: if you block all cookies, some parts of the site (such as the embedded Google Map) may not display correctly.

5. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal. We currently do not respond to DNT signals because there is no universally agreed standard. However, you can always manage cookies through your browser settings as described above.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation or our business practices. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of the page. We encourage you to revisit this page periodically.
