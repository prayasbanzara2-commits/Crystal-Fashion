Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: June 2026 · This policy applies to all visitors and customers of crystalfashion.com.np.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Crystal Fashion ("we", "our", "us"). We are a handcraft fashion studio based in Kathmandu, Nepal, established in 2009. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and safeguard information when you visit our website, purchase our products, or contact us through any channel.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide to us directly, information collected automatically when you use our site, and information from third-party services we rely on.

2.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number when you submit our contact form, place a custom order, or reach out via social media.
  • Order details — shipping address, product preferences, sizing, and any custom-order specifications.
  • Communications — the content of any messages you send us, including via email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or Pinterest.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages viewed, time spent, links clicked, browser type, device type, operating system, screen resolution, and approximate location (derived from your IP address).
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small files stored on your device. See our Cookie Policy for full details.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Formspree — our contact form provider, who processes form submissions on our behalf.
  • Google Maps — when our embedded map loads, Google may collect interaction data.
  • Social platforms — if you contact us via Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest or WhatsApp, those platforms may share basic profile information per their own policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, custom-order requests and customer service messages.
  • To process and ship orders, including handing over necessary details to shipping partners.
  • To send order updates, shipping notifications and (only with consent) occasional newsletters.
  • To improve our website, products and overall customer experience.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on your jurisdiction, we rely on one or more of these lawful bases: your consent, the performance of a contract with you (e.g. fulfilling an order), our legitimate interests in running our business, or compliance with legal obligations.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers — Formspree (contact form), Google (Maps and Fonts), shipping companies, and payment processors where applicable, all bound to handle your data securely.
  • Legal authorities — when required by Nepali law or to defend our legal rights.
  • Business transfers — in the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition or restructuring, with continued protection of your data.

6. International Data Transfers

Our servers and some of our service providers (Formspree, Google) may be located outside Nepal. By using our site you understand that your data may be transferred to and processed in those countries, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

7. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described above — typically for the lifetime of your customer relationship plus a reasonable period afterwards (usually up to 3 years) for legal, tax and accounting reasons. After that, your data is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — request that we erase your personal data.
  • Restriction — limit how we process your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to certain uses, such as direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at crystlfashionnp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including HTTPS encryption, secure form submission through Formspree, and access controls on our internal systems. However, no system is 100% secure; we encourage you to use strong passwords and to inform us immediately of any suspected compromise.

10. Children's Privacy

Our site and products are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to external platforms (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Google Maps). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing your information.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top and, for significant changes, post a notice on our homepage. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
