IMAGES FOLDER INSTRUCTIONS
===========================

1. Copy your Logo.png here as:
   → images/logo.png

2. Copy product photos here:
   → images/products/hemp-tote-1.jpg
   → images/products/leather-bag.jpg
   → (etc.)

3. Copy hero background here:
   → images/backgrounds/hero-himalayan.jpg
   → images/backgrounds/about-bg.jpg

HOW TO ADD HERO BACKGROUND IMAGE:
----------------------------------
Edit css/style.css, find ".hero::before" and add:
    background-image: url('images/backgrounds/hero-himalayan.jpg');

After adding Logo.png, header and footer will automatically show it!
