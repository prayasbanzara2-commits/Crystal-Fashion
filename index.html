Our Story

Rooted in Nepal.
Crafted with Heart.

Founded in 2009 in the cultural heart of Kathmandu, Crystal Fashion was born from a vision to share Nepal's rich textile heritage with the world. We believe fashion is more than clothing — it's culture, identity, and art.

Our artisans hand-craft every piece using traditional Nepali techniques passed down through generations, blending them with contemporary designs that resonate globally.

Our Core Values

  • Excellence – Every product meets the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship
  • Creativity – Blending traditional heritage with modern design innovation
  • Sustainability – Committed to eco-friendly hemp and natural fibers
  • Community – Supporting 50+ local artisans and their families
  • Authenticity – 100% genuine Nepali craftsmanship in every stitch
